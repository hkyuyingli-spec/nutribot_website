What NutriBot Does

NutriBot is an AI-powered wellness assistant that provides educational guidance on Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), herbal nutrition, acupressure, and everyday self-care. Simply ask a question about your wellness goals, daily routines, or traditional remedies, and receive clear, thoughtful guidance grounded in holistic health principles.
