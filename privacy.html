Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Information we collect

When you use the NutriBot chatbot, the information you type — including health, lifestyle, and any DNA-related details you choose to share — is sent to our AI service provider to generate a response. We do not require you to create an account to use the chatbot.

How we use your information

Information shared with NutriBot is used solely to generate relevant responses to your questions. We may use anonymized, aggregated conversation data to improve NutriBot's accuracy and usefulness over time.

Sensitive health information

NutriBot may process sensitive health and genetic information that you voluntarily provide. Please only share information you're comfortable providing, and avoid including identifying details you'd prefer to keep private.

Third-party services

The chatbot is powered by a third-party AI platform. Your messages are processed by that platform in order to generate responses. Please review the privacy practices of that provider for details on how they handle data.

Cookies

This website may use basic cookies to support core functionality. We do not use cookies for advertising tracking.

Contact us
