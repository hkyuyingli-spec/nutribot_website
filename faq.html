FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is NutriBot?

NutriBot is an AI chatbot that gives personalized nutrition and health guidance based on the information you share about your lifestyle, health history, and — if available — your DNA.

Do I need DNA test results to use NutriBot?

No. NutriBot can offer useful guidance based on your lifestyle and health information alone. DNA results, if you have them, simply allow for more tailored suggestions.

Is NutriBot a substitute for a doctor?

No. NutriBot provides general information to support your own decisions, not medical diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns or before changing your diet significantly.

Is my information kept private?

We take privacy seriously. Please see our Privacy Policy for details on how information shared with NutriBot is handled.

Is NutriBot available all the time?

Yes — NutriBot is available 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

How is NutriBot's advice generated?

NutriBot runs on a large language model, refined through ongoing prompt engineering and review, and grounded in nutrigenomic and general nutrition knowledge.

Can my organization partner with NutriBot?

We're open to partnerships and advertising opportunities with organizations interested in health, nutrition, or AI. Reach out via our Contact page.

Still have a question?

Ask NutriBot directly, or get in touch with our team.
