About NutriBot

An AI adviser built around nutrigenomics

NutriBot exists to make personalized nutrition advice accessible. Instead of generic diet tips, NutriBot listens to your health history, day-to-day habits, and — if you choose to share it — your DNA information, and uses that context to suggest nutrition and lifestyle choices that fit you.

The aim is simple: help people improve their wellbeing or manage health concerns through better-informed eating, grounded in the science of how genes and nutrients interact.

How it works

From conversation to guidance

1. You share context

Tell NutriBot about your lifestyle, health goals, or concerns — and your DNA results, if you have them.

2. NutriBot analyzes

NutriBot draws on nutrigenomic principles and general health knowledge to interpret what you've shared.

3. You get guidance

You receive practical, personalized suggestions for nutrition and habits — ready whenever you need them.

Our approach

AI-powered, continuously refined

NutriBot is built on a large language model and refined through ongoing prompt engineering and conversation review, so its responses keep improving over time. A small human team — covering strategy, partnerships, and oversight — works alongside specialized AI assistants that handle day-to-day operations, keeping NutriBot responsive and efficient.

We're always open to partnerships with organizations interested in health, nutrition, or AI — get in touch if you'd like to collaborate.
