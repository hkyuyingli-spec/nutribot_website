How NutriBot can help

Chatbot consultations

Open a chat with NutriBot whenever a nutrition or health question comes up. Describe your situation — your habits, symptoms, or goals — and get a personalized response, day or night, with no appointment needed.

DNA-informed analysis

If you've had genetic testing done, share the relevant results with NutriBot. It can help you understand what those results may mean for your nutrition — for example, how your body processes certain nutrients — and suggest dietary adjustments accordingly.

Traditional Chinese Medicine perspectives

For those interested in TCM concepts like food energetics and balance, NutriBot can discuss your questions through that lens alongside modern nutrigenomic science — giving you a more rounded view of options to consider.

A note on scope

NutriBot provides general nutrition and wellness information to support your own decisions. It does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional. If you have a medical condition or are taking medication, talk to your doctor before making significant dietary changes.
